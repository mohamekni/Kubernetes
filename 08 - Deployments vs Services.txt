Deployments vs Services in Kubernetes

1. Deployment (Application Management)

A Deployment manages Pods (application instances)

- Creates Pods
- Ensures desired number of replicas
- Handles updates (rolling updates)
- Handles rollback
- Self-healing (recreates failed Pods)


2. Service (Network Access)
A Service exposes and connects to Pods.

- Provides stable IP / DNS
- Load balances traffic across Pods
- Enables communication between apps
- Exposes app externally (NodePort / LoadBalancer)
